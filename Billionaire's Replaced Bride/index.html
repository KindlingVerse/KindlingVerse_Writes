Billionaire's Replaced Bride

She was the childhood friend - the girl next door with a hopeless crush on the groom.
He was the charming heir - days away from marrying someone else.

But when the bride runs away just hours before the wedding, fate takes a wild turn.
In a whirlwind decision to protect family honor, Meera finds herself walking down the aisle... not as a guest, but as the bride. What begins as a marriage of convenience turns into stolen glances, late-night confessions, passionate kisses, and a love story no one saw coming - not even them.

But can a marriage built on circumstances grow into a forever kind of love?

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If you're in the mood for a lighthearted romance filled with unexpected twists, a runaway bride, a surprise wedding, and plenty of fun - then this story is just what your heart needs!
