A Love In Penance

Chandni played a ruthless game and won a husband. Aadiv made a noble sacrifice and lost his happiness. Bound by a contract, separated by hatred, they were a perfect tragedy. Fate, however, had a cruel twist in store-one that would force them to discover that the deepest love can be born in the deepest wreckage.
