The Sunflower's Shadow

To her family, she was selfish.
To his, a schemer.
To Alexander Hale, she was a wife he never wanted, met only with glacial contempt.

They crafted a villain from the only innocent among them, blind to the devastating truth of her sacrifice.

Some debts of the heart are paid in secret, and some secrets demand a price no one saw coming.
