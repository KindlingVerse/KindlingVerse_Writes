Whispers of Love (ONESHOT)

She loved him in silence. He judged her from a distance. Fate brought them together-but can love grow in the ashes of misunderstanding?

*************************************************

Arpita has always loved Abhimanyu, the man who sees her as nothing more than a spoiled brat. When his bride elopes and their families pressure them into marriage, she agrees-for his happiness.
But she's not the girl he thought she was.
Quietly, she keeps her distance. Painfully, she loves him from the shadows.
When he begins to see the truth-the warmth behind her silence, the sacrifices she made for him-it may already be too late.

Because sometimes, love doesn't shout.
Sometimes, it whispers... and walks away.
